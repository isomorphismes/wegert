Choose zero or pole, then tap the portrait. Opposite factors cancel at the same point.

Colour

Hue is phase. Lightness is log modulus.

The reusable colour core takes the value of a complex function. Function construction stays upstream. The mapping follows the phase-portrait approach developed by Elias Wegert.
