Wegert is an interactive phase-portrait explorer for complex rational functions.

Tap to add zeros, use two fingers to add poles, drag to pan the complex plane, and pinch to zoom. The phase portrait is rendered directly on the GPU using domain coloring: hue shows phase and repeating lightness bands show modulus.

The Android app is native C with EGL and OpenGL ES 3 and does not require a JavaScript layer or network service.
